FAQ

What clinics ask us.

Who is Clinic Management Tech for?

Aesthetic medicine and aesthetic dermatology clinics, medical spas and practices that treat the face or body and want to connect their team with their patients.

Do I have to replace my current system?

No. We start where your clinic needs it most, and connect or migrate what you already use step by step.

Does the facial analysis replace a medical assessment?

No. It is a support tool to document and explain proportion, symmetry and skin. The assessment and the treatment decision always belong to the doctor.

How does the clinic shop work?

You load the products you sell, with your prices and stock. When the doctor prescribes a routine, each step links to a product, and the patient can buy it in her app for pickup at reception or delivery. Orders, stock and sales appear in your console.

Which brands can I sell?

The ones your clinic already carries or distributes, such as Colorescience, Sensilis, SkinCeuticals, ISDIN or Heliocare, plus your own line. The catalog is yours; we do not sell products to your patients.

How do patients pay?

Online with the payment provider your clinic chooses, or at reception when they pick up. Payment setup depends on your country and is part of onboarding.

What does Savia do?

Savia talks to your patients: she explains their analysis, sends aftercare and routine guidance, reminds refills, books check-ups and flags anything urgent to your team. She never gives a diagnosis, and your team can step into any conversation.

Can I use my own brand?

Yes. The app and the shop carry your clinic's identity so your patients recognize them as yours.
